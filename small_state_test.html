The Decision Room · Episode 1Small State Survival Guide

Stuck between great powers, which leader are you?

Sixteen files land on your desk. Each one needs a decision. At the end you get a dossier: the real leader of a small country who played it the way you did, and what happened to them. About four minutes.

  • AccommodateGive the giant next door what it needs to feel safe
  • HedgeGet strong, then make every power need you
  • Walk the tightropeNo army to speak of, just your own diplomacy
  • Find a patronSide with the giant's rival
  • Refuse both campsSay no to each bloc and build a third
  • Bend and reformGive up the edges, keep the core, modernise fast
  • Trust the guaranteesTreaties, allies and international law
  • FortressTrust no one and seal the borders

There are no right answers. Every one of these routes was tried by someone, with very different endings.
